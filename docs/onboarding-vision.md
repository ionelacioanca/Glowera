# Glowera Onboarding Vision

## Purpose

The onboarding experience should not feel like filling out a form.

It should feel like entering a new world.

The goal is to create an emotional connection between the user and the Glowera universe before asking for personal information. Users should first understand where they are, who will guide them, and how Glowera can help them.

Only after this introduction should we begin collecting information about the user.


## Phase 1 — Welcome to Glowera

The onboarding begins with the Glowera planet.

This is the user's first encounter with the universe.

The planet slowly rotates while introducing the concept of Glowera as a place where different aspects of personal growth coexist and work together.

The objective is to spark curiosity and create a sense of discovery.


## Phase 2 — Discover the Regions

The user is guided through each region of the planet.

### Glowderma

The region dedicated to skincare, self-care routines, and skin health.

### Glowlunara

The region dedicated to cycle tracking, hormonal wellness, and understanding the body's natural rhythms.

### Glowvitalia

The region dedicated to physical and mental wellbeing, energy, nutrition, and overall health.

### Glowmedula

The region dedicated to hair care, hair goals, and hair health.

### Glowdiario

The region dedicated to habits, journaling, personal growth, and everyday progress.

Each region should feel like a unique destination with its own personality while remaining part of the same world.


## Phase 3 — Meet Glowria

After exploring the planet, the user meets Glowria.

Glowria is not just an assistant.

She is the resident guide of Glowera.

Her role is to help users navigate the different regions, understand their goals, stay motivated, and celebrate progress.

This moment establishes trust and introduces the relationship between the user and Glowria.


## Phase 4 — Let's Get to Know You

Only after the user understands the world do we begin personalization.

Glowria asks for information in a friendly and conversational way.

The purpose is not to collect data for the sake of data, but to understand the person behind the profile.

Potential information may include:

* Preferred name
* Primary goals
* Areas of interest
* Wellness priorities
* Current challenges
* Desired outcomes

The experience should feel like a conversation rather than a questionnaire.


## Phase 5 — Your First Destination

Based on the user's answers, Glowria recommends a starting region.

This becomes the user's first destination inside Glowera.

The recommendation should feel personalized and meaningful.

Examples:

* A user focused on skincare may begin in Glowderma.
* A user focused on habit building may begin in Glowdiario.
* A user focused on cycle awareness may begin in Glowlunara.


## Desired Feeling

When onboarding is complete, the user should feel:

* Understood
* Motivated
* Curious to explore
* Guided rather than instructed
* Excited to begin their journey

The onboarding should feel less like creating an account and more like arriving in a new world designed specifically for them.
